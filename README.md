## Summary

Live for each other and die for each other, it's a team game so share the pain together.
This mod aims to have players work together by combining many different resource pools such as health, block, some powers and some relics.
More specifically it combines every players health bar into one massive health bar and when players block, it blocks for everyone.
Enemies also do n times damage to offset the higher block of everyone.

## General Changes

Health bars are combined. For example, in a game with two Ironclads, their health bars would be 160 (80 + 80) each.

Health bars are synced together. To do this, whenever a player takes damage or heals, all other players take that same amount or damage or healing too.
Whenever players gain Max HP or lose Max HP all other health bars are increased or decreased as well.

Block is shared. When one player blocks 5, all other players also block 5 without playing another card.

Enemy damage is multiplied by the number of players. This is to balance out the abundance of block enabled by the sharing between all players.

Osty summon is shared with everyone. This is so that block and damage taken between players is still synced up.

## Specific Changes

### Relics
All the following Relic effects are shared among players so that block/damage taken/health is synced up
- Beating Remnant
- Paper Krane
- Sturdy Clamp
- Tungsten rod
- Undying sigil
- Diamond Diadem
  - the total cards played between all players has to be less than or equal to 2 times the number of players in order for all players to receive the damage reduction

### Powers/Buffs/Debuffs

n refers to the number of players

On Enemies:
- Strength is multiplied by n
  - This means that powers like Ritual, Territorial, Enrage, or Suck give n times more strength than it says
- Vigor is multiplied by n
- #### Waterfall Giant
  - Steam Eruption is multiplied by n
- #### The Insatiable
  - Sandpit starts with n times the normal amount
  - Playing Frantic escape increases sandpit for all players
  - Each turn, Sandpit decreases by n for every player

On Players:
- Doom is shared
- Frail from Shame applies to everyone
- NoBlock (Panic button debuff)
- TheGambit
- WraithForm
- #### Knowledge Demon
  - Disintegration is shared
- #### Infested Prism
  - Tainted is shared

The following is only shared due to trying to sync block/damage taken/health and I realize that these will be more overpowered in this mod.
- Barricade is shared
- Buffer is shared
- Colossus is shared
- Intangible is shared
- Blur is shared

### Multiplayer Cards

These cards have been removed either because they go against the point of the mod or simply they are just too op.

Colorless Cards:
- Beacon of Hope
- Intercept
- Rally

Ironclad:
- Tank

Necrobinder:
- Legion of Bone

Note: Legion of Bone and Rally gives summon and block to every player. It can either just give everyone the normal amount (everyone gets 4 summon/12 block) or give everyone n times the amount so that it actually has a use in this mod.

The cards are either practically useless (Legion of Bone is strictly worse than Bodyguard and Rally is basically just 2 Defends) or incredibly overpowered (Unleash damage goes crazy or just a ton of block).
